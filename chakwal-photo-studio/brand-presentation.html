The logo

Darwaza: the doorway mark

The idea

Every family photograph is a doorway back home

  • The focus frame فوکسThe four corner marks every camera shows when it locks focus. It says "photography" without using another camera icon. The studio's promise: we focus on your moment.
  • The doorway دروازہThe arched door of a Punjabi home, the arch of a wedding stage and the shape of a framed portrait on the wall. It stands for family, welcome and the house where the photos hang.
  • Sunrise over the Potohar پوٹھوہارThe flat-topped ridges of the Potohar plateau around Chakwal, with the sun rising behind them. A new beginning: a wedding, a newborn, a first day at school. Gold is used only for the sun.

Aap ke ghar ki yaadein, Chakwal ki mitti se. A trusted local studio, drawn with the quality of a leading one.

Design process

Three concepts, one direction

Each concept was tested against the studio's real uses: a signboard read from a moving rickshaw on Pindi Road, a 40-pixel WhatsApp profile, and a gold stamp on a wedding album.

Concept A · Emotional

Naslein (Generations)

Three nested arches: grandparents, parents and the child at the centre, in gold. Like portraits from three generations hanging in one frame.

Strongest feeling of family. On its own it does not say "photography" or "Chakwal".

Concept B · Photography + place

Potohar Lens

A lens aperture whose opening shows the Potohar ridge and a rising sun. The landscape seen through the camera.

Clear photography read and real local reference. The aperture is common in the trade and goes muddy at small sizes.

Concept C · Typographic

CPS Heritage Seal

A round seal with a C·P·S monogram and the name set around the ring, like an old studio's embossing stamp.

Premium and classic, but small ring text fails on signboards and phones, and it feels distant.

Usage

Three rules for the printer and the designer

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the "C" in CHAKWAL. No text, borders or photos inside it.

Minimum size

Horizontal logo: 30 mm wide in print, 160 px on screen. Below that, use the mark alone (8 mm / 24 px).

Do not change

Do not recolour the sun, stretch the logo, add shadows or glow, or retype the name in another font. Use the supplied files only.
