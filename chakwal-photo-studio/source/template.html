The idea

A camera that opens like the door of home

{{MARK}}
  • The camera کیمرہA clear camera, so anyone passing on Pindi Road knows at once that this is a photo studio. It reads on the signboard, on a WhatsApp profile and even at rubber-stamp size.
  • The doorway دروازہThe top of the camera is an arch: the door of a Punjabi home and the arch of a wedding stage. It stands for family, welcome and celebration.
  • Sunrise in the lens پوٹھوہارThrough the lens: the flat-topped ridges of the Potohar plateau around Chakwal, with the sun rising behind them. A new beginning: a wedding, a newborn, a family together. Gold is used only for the sun.

Aap ke ghar ki yaadein, Chakwal ki mitti se. A trusted local studio, drawn with the quality of a leading one.

Usage

Rules and files

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the "C" in CHAKWAL. No text, borders or photos inside it.

Minimum size

Horizontal logo: 30 mm wide in print, 160 px on screen. Below that, use the mark alone (8 mm / 24 px).

Do not change

Do not recolour the sun, stretch the logo, add shadows or glow, or retype the name in another font. Use the supplied files only.
